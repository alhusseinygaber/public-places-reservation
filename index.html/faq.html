Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a venue?

Browse the places, select a venue, choose a date and time, specify the number of visitors, and complete the payment.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, you can cancel active bookings from the My Bookings page up to 24 hours before the scheduled time.

What payment methods are supported?

We support Meeza, electronic wallets, and credit/debit cards.

How do I change my profile information?

Go to the Profile page and edit your details or change your password.
